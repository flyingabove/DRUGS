# Competitive Intelligence Analysis Framework

Systematic framework for analyzing competitive landscape in drug discovery.

## Analysis Dimensions

### 1. Market Overview

**Key Metrics:**
| Metric | Description | Sources |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Approved Products | Number of approved drugs | FDA/EMA, DrugBank |
| Pipeline Count | Drugs in development | ClinicalTrials.gov |
| Market Size | Current and forecasted sales | Evaluate, IQVIA |
| Growth Rate | Annual market growth | Market reports |
| Key Players | Major companies | Company databases |

**Analysis Questions:**
- Is the market established or emerging?
- What is the competitive density?
- Are there clear market leaders?
- What are the barriers to entry?

### 2. Competitive Positioning

**Positioning Framework:**
```
         High Differentiation
                ↑
                |
     Niche      |      Leader
                |
Low ←──────────┼──────────→ High Market Share
                |
     Follower   |      Challenged
                |
                ↓
         Low Differentiation
```

**Categories:**
| Quadrant | Characteristics | Strategy |
|----------|-----------------|----------|
| Leader | High share, high diff | Defend position |
| Challenger | Low share, high diff | Grow aggressively |
| Niche | Low share, low diff | Focus on segment |
| Follower | High share, low diff | Cost leadership |

### 3. Pipeline Analysis

**By Development Phase:**
```
Phase I      →  Proof of concept, safety
Phase II     →  Efficacy, dose finding
Phase III    →  Pivotal trials
Regulatory   →  Review process
Approved     →  Marketed
```

**Pipeline Health Indicators:**
| Metric | Healthy | Concerning |
|--------|---------|------------|
| Phase transition | >60% advance | <40% advance |
| Trial duration | On schedule | Significant delays |
| Enrollment | Meeting targets | Slow recruitment |
| Regulatory | Approvals | Rejections/CRLs |

### 4. Mechanism of Action Analysis

**MOA Classification:**
```
Inhibition
  ├── Receptor antagonism
  ├── Enzyme inhibition
  └── Channel blockade

Activation
  ├── Receptor agonism
  ├── Enzyme activation
  └── Channel opening

Modulation
  ├── Allosteric modulation
  ├── Partial agonism
  └── Functional selectivity

Novel
  ├── Degradation (PROTAC)
  ├── Gene therapy
  └── Cell therapy
```

### 5. Differentiation Assessment

**Differentiation Dimensions:**

| Dimension | Questions | Scoring |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Efficacy | Superior outcomes? | 1-5 |
| Safety | Better profile? | 1-5 |
| Convenience | Dosing/formulation? | 1-5 |
| Cost | Price advantage? | 1-5 |
| Novelty | First-in-class? | 1-5 |

**Differentiation Score = Sum × 4 (max 100)**

### 6. Company Analysis

**Assessment Criteria:**
```
Financial Resources
  ├── R&D budget
  ├── Market cap
  └── Cash position

Technical Capabilities
  ├── Platform technologies
  ├── IP portfolio
  └── Development expertise

Commercial Strength
  ├── Market access
  ├── Sales force
  └── Brand presence

Strategic Focus
  ├── Core vs. opportunistic
  ├── Partnership approach
  └── Acquisition history
```

## Analysis Best Practices

### 1. Data Quality
- Verify from multiple sources
- Check publication dates
- Understand methodology
- Document assumptions

### 2. Objectivity
- Avoid confirmation bias
- Consider contrarian views
- Scenario planning
- Sensitivity analysis

### 3. Timeliness
- Set up alerts for key assets
- Monitor conferences
- Track company news
- Review quarterly updates

### 4. Presentation
- Executive summary first
- Visualizations (tables, charts)
- Clear recommendations
- Actionable insights

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Impact | Mitigation |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Outdated data | Wrong decisions | Verify dates, set alerts |
| Confirmation bias | Missed risks | Challenge assumptions |
| Incomplete scope | Blind spots | Comprehensive review |
| Misinterpretation | Wrong conclusions | Peer review |
| Over-analysis | Delayed decisions | Set timeboxes |
